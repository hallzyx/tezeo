01

Concept & voice

Tezeo = tejer (to weave) + -eo, the Spanish suffix for a rhythm that never stops (goteo, tecleo, zapateo). The steady weaving of small acts of support. The z is a zig-zag stitch.

Personality

Warm, like a neighbour— not like a bank
Crafted— handmade details, never ornate
Calm under pressure— errors are explained, never blamed
Proudly Latin American— the region's colour, not a stereotype
