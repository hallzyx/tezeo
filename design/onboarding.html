Step 3 of 3 · Where tips land

Where should your tips land?

Paste your Solana wallet address. Tips arrive there as USDC, digital dollars, straight from your fans. Tezeo never holds your money.

Copy it from your wallet app and paste it. Typing it by hand is how mistakes happen.

Where do I find my Solana address?
  1. Open the Phantom app.
  2. Tap Receive.
  3. Find Solana in the list and tap Copy.
  4. Come back here and tap Paste.

Phantom also shows Ethereum and other addresses. Use the one next to Solana.

  1. Open the Solflare app.
  2. Tap Receive.
  3. Tap Copy under your address.
  4. Come back here and tap Paste.
  1. Go to Deposit and choose USDC.
  2. Pick the Solana network.
  3. Copy the address and paste it here.

If the exchange also shows a memo or tag, use a personal wallet instead. Tezeo can't add a memo, so tips might not reach your account.

No wallet yet? Phantom and Solflare are free apps, and setting one up takes a few minutes. Write down the recovery phrase it gives you and keep it to yourself.

  • Never paste these
  • Your secret recovery phrase (12 or 24 words). No one at Tezeo will ever ask for it.
  • An Ethereum address, the kind that starts with 0x.
  • A coin's address, like USDC's own. It has to be your wallet.
